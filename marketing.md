# MonoTask: Marketing & Distribution Strategy

You don't need a huge budget. You need to be where the "Overwhelmed" people are.

## 1. The Target Channels
- **Reddit (The Gold Mine):**
  - r/ADHD, r/Productivity, r/SmallStepSucceed.
  - *Strategy:* Don't post an ad. Post a story: *"I built this for myself because I was tired of $30/mo apps that felt like a second job. It breaks my tasks into 2-min wins and has a 'Potato Energy' mode."*
- **TikTok/Reels (The "Vibe" Channel):**
  - *Strategy:* Show, don't tell. Post 15-second "satisfying" videos of the "Zen Jar" filling up or a task "popping." Use tags like #ADHDhacks and #QuietLuxury.
- **Product Hunt:**
  - Launch here on a Tuesday. The "Linen" aesthetic will stand out against the usual blue/purple tech apps.

## 2. The "First 100" Plan
- **Offer a "Founding Member" Lifetime Deal:** $49 for life (instead of $5/mo). This gets you $4,900 upfront to pay for your domain and any future API costs.
- **The "ADHD Tax" Calculator:** Create a free tool on your landing page that calculates how much money they lose to forgotten subscriptions and late fees. Then offer MonoTask as the "tax-free" solution.

## 3. The Referral Loop
- Give users a "Free Month" if they refer a friend. 
- *Why:* People with ADHD often find community in their struggles. If one person finds a tool that works, they will tell their whole Discord/friend group.
