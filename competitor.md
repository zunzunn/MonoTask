## 2. Our "Unfair Advantages" (What makes us stand out)

### A. The "Potato Energy" Toggle (Absolute Winner)
**Competitors** assume you are a high-performing robot every day. 
**MonoTask** is the only app that has a "Low Energy" mode. When you toggle it, the AI simplifies the language, hides complex tasks, and suggests "1-minute wins." This builds **trust** with the user because the app "understands" their struggle.

### B. Subtractive UI (Tunnel Vision)
Most apps show you a sidebar, a calendar, a list, and a chat. **MonoTask** shows you **ONE** thing. We are the only app brave enough to hide your entire to-do list so you can actually finish one task.

### C. The "Zen Jar" vs. "XP"
Gamification usually feels like "AI Slop" (Badges, levels). MonoTask uses **Tactile Rewards**. Completing a task feels like popping a real bubble. Seeing your jar fill with sparks feels like a physical achievement. It’s "Cozy Productivity."

## 3. Pricing Strategy vs. Competitors
- **Motion:** $19–$34/mo (Way too expensive for most consumers).
- **Sunsama:** $20/mo (Hard to justify for manual work).
- **MonoTask:** **$4.99/mo.**
  - *Positioning:* "The price of one coffee for a month of mental clarity." 
  - *Why:* It’s low enough to be an "impulse buy" but high enough to cover your AI costs and turn a profit.

## 4. Conclusion
MonoTask stands out by being the **"Gentlest"** and **"Cleanest"** tool in a market filled with "High-Pressure" AI tools. We don't try to manage the user's life; we just help them finish the **next** thing.
