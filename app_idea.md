# MonoTask: Product Strategy & The "Dopamine Engine"

## 1. The Core Problem
Most productivity apps are **Additive**. They ask you to *add* tasks, *add* tags, *add* deadlines. For a brain struggling with Executive Dysfunction, this is just more "noise." 

**MonoTask is Subtractive.** It takes the noise and gives you back **Silence**.

---

## 2. Key Differentiators
1. **The Decomposition Engine:** Instead of you thinking about *how* to do something, you just say what you want. The AI does the cognitive heavy lifting of breaking it into 2-minute "micro-wins."
2. **"Potato Energy" Mode:** A toggle that changes the UI and the AI's tone. It recognizes that some days you aren't a "High Performer," and that's okay.
3. **Digital Fidgeting:** Integrated "calm zones" (draggable pebbles, interactive sand) to keep the user on-page during moments of distraction.

---

## 3. The Dopamine Engine: "The Bloom & The Jar"
*Brutally honest take: XP, Levels, and Badges are "AI Slop" gamification. They feel fake and quickly become a burden.*

**The MonoTask Solution: Tactile, Visual Evolution.**

### A. The "Pop" (Instant Reward)
When a sub-task is completed, it shouldn't just be a checkmark.
- **Visual:** The task "pearl" pops into a cloud of color.m
- **Haptic/Sound:** A crisp, high-quality "bubble pop" sound (recorded from real objects, not synthesized).
- **Why it works:** It’s a sensory "micro-dose" of satisfaction.

### B. The "Zen Jar" (Medium-Term Reward)
- Every completed task adds a "Spark" (a small, glowing orb) into a glass jar at the bottom corner of the screen.
- The sparks are physically simulated—they bounce around and glow.
- **The Payoff:** When the jar is full (e.g., 5 tasks), it "releases." The screen undergoes a 10-second "Transition of Beauty"—a high-end Lottie animation of a garden blooming or a star forming.
- **The "Real" Reward:** Once the jar is released, the user is offered a **Curated Break**: a 5-minute link to something genuinely interesting (not social media), like a high-def drone video of a forest or an interactive art site.

### C. The "Bloom" (Long-Term Reward)
- Your dashboard isn't a list; it’s a **Garden**.
- For every 10 tasks completed, a new, unique hand-drawn plant (Lottie) is added to your permanent "Garden" page.
- **Why it works:** It’s a visual representation of "Time spent well" that doesn't feel like a high-score table. It feels like an achievement of calm.

---

## 4. Why People Will Pay $5/mo
People pay for **Mental Clarity**. 
- They aren't paying for a to-do list; they are paying for a **Cognitive Assistant** that stops them from feeling like a failure.
- The $5 price point makes it an "impulse buy" for students and young professionals who spend more than that on a single coffee to try and "focus."
